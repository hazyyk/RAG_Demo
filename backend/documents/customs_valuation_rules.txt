Article 1: Exchange Rate for Customs Valuation (Part A, Section VII)
The rate of exchange between Vietnam dong and a foreign currency used for dutiable value determination is the average transaction exchange rate on the interbank foreign currency market publicized by the State Bank of Vietnam at the time of tax calculation, published on Nhan Dan daily and the State Bank's website. If a taxpayer declares before the customs declaration registration date, the exchange rate on the declaration date applies, provided it is no more than three days earlier than the registration date.

Article 2: Tax-Free Thresholds for Gifts and Presents to Individuals (Part D, Section I.4.2.2)
Gifts or presents given by overseas organizations or individuals to Vietnamese individuals are exempt from import tax if their value does not exceed one million Vietnam dong, or if the value exceeds one million dong but the total payable tax amount would be under fifty thousand dong. Exemption in these cases does not require going through import tax exemption consideration procedures.

Article 3: Tax-Free Thresholds for Gifts and Presents to Organizations (Part D, Section I.4.2.1)
Gifts or presents given by overseas organizations or individuals to Vietnamese organizations, such as State agencies, political organizations, or social organizations, are considered for tax exemption if their value does not exceed thirty million Vietnam dong.

Article 4: Refund for Goods Re-Exported to a Foreign Owner or Third Country (Part E, Section I.8)
Imported goods that must be re-exported to their foreign owner or to a third country are considered for a refund of the paid import tax, provided the goods are re-exported within one year (rounded to 365 days) after actual importation, have not gone through production, processing, repair, or use in Vietnam, and the re-export goes through customs procedures at the place where import procedures were originally carried out.

Article 5: Complaints Against Tax Decisions (Part K, Section I)
The powers and responsibilities of individuals and organizations to complain about import tax and export tax, and the responsibilities and powers of agencies handling such complaints, are governed by the Law on Import Tax and Export Tax and related legal provisions.
