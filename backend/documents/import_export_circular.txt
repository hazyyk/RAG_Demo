Article 1: Taxable Objects (Part A, Section I)
Goods exported or imported through Vietnamese border gates or border are liable to import tax or export tax, including goods exported or imported through border gates on land, rivers, at seaports, airports, transnational railways, international posts, and other customs procedure clearance venues established under decisions of competent state agencies. Goods brought from the domestic market into non-tariff zones and vice versa are also liable to this tax.

Article 2: Temporary Import for Re-Export by Foreign Contractors on ODA Projects (Part D, Section I.18)
Machinery, equipment, and means of transport imported by foreign contractors into Vietnam by the mode of temporary import for re-export, for use in constructing works or projects funded with official development assistance (ODA), are exempt from import tax and export tax upon re-export. At the end of the construction period, foreign contractors must re-export these goods; if a contractor instead wishes to sell or liquidate such goods in Vietnam, it must obtain permission from a competent state agency and declare the goods for import tax payment.

Article 3: Export Tax Payment Time Limit (Part C, Section III.1)
For exported goods, the export tax payment time limit is thirty days from the date taxpayers register their customs declarations.

Article 4: Import Tax Payment Time Limit for Compliant Taxpayers (Part C, Section III.2.2 and III.2.2.1)
Taxpayers who have well observed customs law and owe no overdue tax debts or fines at the time of customs declaration registration are considered compliant taxpayers. For imported supplies or raw materials used directly in producing goods for export, compliant taxpayers are allowed a tax payment time limit of two hundred seventy-five days from the date of customs declaration registration.
